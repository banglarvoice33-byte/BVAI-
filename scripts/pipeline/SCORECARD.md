# BVAI Shadow Scorecard (Non-blocking)

## Rules
- Score only. Never block publish.
- 1 video = 1 row.
- Missing evidence = 0 for that row.

## Score (100)
- Claim + source: 30
- Visual date/place/subject/source: 25
- Bangla pronunciation: 20
- Audio -16 LUFS, music -15 dB: 10
- Caption = narration: 10
- Disclosure + [archive] label: 5

## Log format
date | video_id | claim | visual | bangla | audio | caption | disclosure | total | notes
